Privacy Policy for InsurMantra

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

Welcome to InsurMantra. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document describes how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Storage

InsurMantra is designed to help insurance agents manage clients, policies, claims, and renewal reminders effectively.

  • Local Data Storage: All personal data entered into the app (such as client names, contact details, policy numbers, and renewal dates) is primarily stored locally on your device using a secure local database (Room Database).
  • Cloud Sync (Optional): If you choose to enable Firebase Cloud Backup/Sync, your encrypted data will be stored securely on Google Firebase servers to allow multi-device sync and restoration.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or deliver targeted advertisements:

  • Google Play Services: Required for application stability, essential runtime functionality, and app updates.
  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the application. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or contextual ads.
  • Firebase / Firestore: Used for optional cloud backup and authentication services.

3. Permissions Required

The application may request certain permissions to provide seamless features:

  • Internet Access: Required to fetch dynamic policy quotes, perform cloud sync, and display AdMob advertisements.
  • Notifications: Used to alert you about upcoming policy renewals, client follow-ups, and custom tasks.

4. Data Protection & Security

We value your trust in providing us your information. We strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Children's Privacy

InsurMantra does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
