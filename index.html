Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

Welcome to InsurMantra. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and client records. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide insurance management and portfolio tracking services, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Email address, User ID (UID), and authentication tokens provided when creating an account or logging in via Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication.
  • Insurance & Client Records: Client details (names, phone numbers, policy numbers, premium amounts, due dates, renewal statuses, and claim details) entered by you into the app.
  • Device & Analytics Data: IP address, device identifiers, app usage statistics, and crash logs collected automatically via Firebase Analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • To synchronize client portfolios and policies across your authorized devices via Google Cloud Firestore.
  • To generate policy renewal alerts, payment reminders, and policy summaries.
  • To authenticate user sessions securely and maintain account privacy.
  • To display localized non-personalized or personalized advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • To detect and fix crashes and improve overall app stability.

3. Third-Party Services

InsurMantra integrates with trusted third-party SDKs that may collect data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics)
  • Google AdMob

4. Data Storage and Protection

Your data is stored locally on your device using encrypted Room SQLite database caching and synced securely to Google Cloud Firestore servers using SSL/TLS encryption. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or client data to third-party marketers.

5. Account and Data Deletion Policy (Google Play Compliance)

You have full ownership and control over your data. In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, we provide immediate self-service options inside the application:

  • Wipe All Client & Policy Data: In Settings > Account Settings, you can select "Wipe All My Data" to permanently erase all client profiles, policies, claims, and tasks from both your device and Firebase Cloud servers while keeping your login account active.
  • Delete Account & Cloud Backup: In Settings > Account Settings, you can select "Delete Account & Cloud Data Permanently" to completely delete your account authentication profile and all synced cloud records.
  • Manual Request: You may also request complete data and account removal by emailing us at yogeshloharana@gmail.com. All data will be permanently wiped within 7 business days of request verification.

6. Children's Privacy

InsurMantra is intended for licensed insurance advisors and financial planners. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
